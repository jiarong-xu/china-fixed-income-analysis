# Fixed Income Model

[Download the Excel model](China_Fixed_Income_Model.xlsx)

The workbook includes bond cash-flow schedules, accrued interest, yield-to-maturity calculations, a yield-to-put scenario, price–yield curves, and a Sino-Ocean Capital credit-event chronology.

Historical price observations relate to March 2023. Sources and modelling assumptions are stated beside the relevant inputs.
